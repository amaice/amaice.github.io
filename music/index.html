Music Portfolio

Hi everyone! I have been composing since 2019 and have written music in many settings and styles. My biggest inspirations are (jazz) Wayne Shorter, Bill Evans, Joao Gilberto, Gil Evans, (classical) Frederic Chopin, Nikolai Kapustin, (modern) Louis Cole, Snail's House, (vgm) Animal Crossing, and honestly, just all of Nintendo's music.

Like Snow - 2026

Large Ensemble, Modern Jazz

The last piece I was able to write for my university's jazz combo! This time a feature for soprano saxophone by great musician and friend Matt Thompson. I very quickly wrote the main musical material, a simple 16 bars with a melody based around a falling perfect fourth (inspired by modern tunes like E.S.P, Maiden Voyage, Dolphin Dance, and Nefertiti).

Around this time is when I realized just how incredible of a writer Wayne Shorter was. Both Dave Liebman and Jazz at the Lincoln Center have great tribute albums. (If you listen to the Dave Liebman one you'll see how much I "stole" lol).

Loro - 2025

Large Ensemble, Ballad

Loro is a feature for flugelhorn. An instrument best described as a mix between trumpet and horn, and 10x as hard to play in tune. A lot of music inspired me for this tune: Crystal Silence, Dienda, Miles Ahead and Infant Eyes most notably. I'm quite proud of this arrangement, especially the ending - with how it subtly grows during the flugel solo, then explodes into the melody, then sweetly ends in duet.

Loro means parrot in Spanish! I didn't write this piece with birds in mind though. I'm bad with titles, I like to write music and then name them after ^^

Archangel's Sight - 2024

Large Ensemble, Modern Jazz

The second piece I wrote for my combo. The piece is divided into two main sections, the beginning Eb lydian section and the ending vamp that sticks with two chords, Ebmaj7#11 and Em11, which works because they both share 3 crucial notes (G, A, and D).

My main inspirations were Asuka Kakitani (particularly her chart, Dragonfly's Glasses, a modern jazz arrangment of an old Japanese folk tune) and Louis Cole (who inspired the ending and some of the grooves).

im trying my best - 2024

Silly Jazz, Vaguely Pixar-y

The first piece I wrote for my jazz combo! Quite daunting and a LOT of work, but highly educational and rewarding. Easily my worst of my 4 combo pieces, but still highly charming and fun. My main complaint is the structure. The many style changes are intentional, but it doesn't feel cohesive or directional, but meandering.

I structured it as a Theme and Variation! The piece goes through many styles but maintains that main melody that you can most clearly hear at the beginning. Can you hear it in the other sections?

Cat Dam - 2026

Ambient, Borderline Horror

A game I developed solo in 7 hours for the 201st Mini Jam, hosted by ZahranW. The game is a tense sprint where you hurridly try to keep up with repairs and prevent the city dam from collapsing.

I wrote the music to be almost unsettling but driving to match this vibe!

Cotton Antivirus - 2025

8-bit, Groove-based

Created in a week for the 6th 1-bit jam. Cotton Antivirus is a Paper Please like game, where you must work against a clock and process files according to your protocol.

I wanted to write something that would not become annoying after looping for the 20th time. So, taking inspiration from Balatro, the theme is in odd meter (5/4).

Mito's Island - 2024

Mario Kart, Retro, Samba

My friend, Femboy Pumpkin, makes some great custom Mario Kart tracks. This was a fun project, him as the game designer, and me writing the music. The track is formed in the shape of Mito the cat, a beloved, polygonal cat I designed many years ago.

This tune has some similiarities to Daisy Circuit Theme (unintentional) and the Brasil '66 arrangement of Tristeza (intentional). Some of the instruments are directly from Mario Kart: Super Circuit, and some are from Animal Crossing!

Mythic Technics - 2026

Electronic, Chill Beats, Soundtrack

It's early in progress, but I have this idea for a visual novel. A young, alcoholic, cyborg lesbian in a large dystopian city, still reeling from her breakup, learns of her ex's disappearance. With this news, she infiltrates the circles that her ex frequented and discovers way more than she intended to.

A lot of visual novels inspired me for this. Loner Dog, Disco Elysium, ZATO, and Oneshot to name a few.

Hilbert the Frog - 2026

Digital Orchestra, Soundtrack, Background Music

Music written for my animated series and subsequent rhythm game, Hilbert The Frog. It's a story about a chef frog and his journey to win the Annual Cooking Competition! Some of the music on this album is quite old, with some demos dating back to 2022, and most tracks being written in 2024. Sostenuto started as a voice memo in 2025, and then was fully arranged in 2026.

I love background music for movies and animes. I was inspired by Joe Hisashi and the soundtracks of slice of life animes - like Lucky Stars, K-On, and Love Live.
