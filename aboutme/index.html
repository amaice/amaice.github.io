Amaice Studios Inc. (often stylized lowercase, as amaice studios), founded in 2019 is an Italian animation studio based in Florence, Italy that has captured the hearts of many around the globe with its vivid worlds and charming characters. Amaice studios is the home to beloved IPs such as Hilbert the Frog, ball, and Just Caught 'Em.
